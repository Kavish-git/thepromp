WHO WE ARE

Driven by Curiosity, Engineered for Impact

We are a collegiate software studio bridging theoretical computer science with real-world, industry-standard engineering.

Our Student Background & Mission

Founded in 2024 by four university computer science students from UC Berkeley, Stanford, and Carnegie Mellon, Synapse Collective was born out of 48-hour competitive hackathons and a shared obsession with high-performance software craft.

Instead of building toy projects, we engineer production-grade web applications, autonomous AI agent pipelines, and distributed cloud services that solve actual bottlenecks for researchers, startups, and university communities.
